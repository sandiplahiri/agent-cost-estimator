## Current limits

- Daily-user volume entry, conditional model-call steps, and DAG agent-call propagation are implemented; explicit manual monthly volume is also supported. Automatic business-volume mapping is not.
- The sensitivity calculation API uses the local price snapshot and does not persist proposed inputs or include additional costs in its LLM delta. Dashboard now shows the ranked cost list without a preview form. Workload and execution edits are available in Agent inventory and the workflow editor.
- Agent links are planning averages. The app rejects cycles; bounded loop execution and a full call-graph runtime are not implemented. The `Agent links` workbook sheet shows formula contributions from frozen parent volumes, while derived row volumes and loaded step costs are precomputed. Change links or upstream volumes in the app and re-export. Importing `Agents` together with populated `Step options` reconstructs exclusive workflows and derived volumes; JSON preserves the complete estimate snapshot.
- Pricing refresh is implemented with snapshot preservation and a before/after preview; live network refresh and every provider's billing rules were not covered by the deterministic E2E run.
- Catalog pricing is community-maintained. Models with missing or recognized unsupported prices remain visibly incomplete. Multimodal, hosted tool, storage, priority/flex, and contract-specific charges may require explicit additional items or custom rates.
- The 300-agent test verifies aggregate budgeting; it is not a performance benchmark for 1,000 individually expanded rows.
- Member descriptions generated during quick setup are explicitly marked pending and require the architect to replace them with real use cases. Counted members share group cost assumptions until individually customized. Identity expansion is capped at 5,000 agents per estimate.
- No live agent execution/monitoring, accounts, or cloud deployment is included.
